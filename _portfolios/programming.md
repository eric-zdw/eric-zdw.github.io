## CHROMA+ELEKTRON

[Link (Steam)](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1020550/CHROMAELEKTRON/)

CHROMA+ELEKTRON is a fast-paced 2D platformer-shooter. Selected for Hand Eye Society's Super FESTival 2025 showcase.  

As the solo developer of the game, I created the concept, game design, programming, visuals, sound effects, music and marketing materials.

### Concept

CHROMA+ELEKTRON

A key chracteristic is that the game is abstract, with no explicit expression of narrative.

Key to the gameplay loop are its two main resources: Color and Energy. Every enemy drops a color powerup; when the player picks it up, its abilities change corresponding to its color. 

More drastically, the amount of Energy stored affects the speed at which the game runs. At high levels of Energy, the game can run as fast as double speed.

It's this interaction between these two resources and the rest of the system where the game receives much of its complexity and novel decision-making, and these interactions permeate through much of the experience of playing the game. One objective was to interlock the systems so closely, have them affect as many variables as I could, that I could no longer reliably predict how the game would behave in specific cases.

### Level Design

At the beginning of a level, the player is required to reach a powerup that will trigger the start of the level. During this period, there is no music and players are encouraged to explore the layout of the level.

Finally, 

Because it's easier for players to drop to lower platforms than it is to jump higher, players will tend towards the lower areas of a level. Levels were designed either to compensate for this characteristic or emphasized as a challenge. In "Scaffold", the defining level element is a tower containing a jump pad that immediately launches the player to the top of the level. Since this is the easiest method of reaching the top of the level, the player's movements centralize around the tower at the bottom of the level, while being more free in decisions regarding how to traverse the level back down to the bottom. In "Stillwater", the fluctuating water levels forces a player to rise in altitude with the tide.

### Visuals and Shader Graph

The primary visual strategy is to use primitive shapes along with heavy use of particle effects. The advantages to this visual style is three-fold:
1. My ability to draw is limited, so using shapes and particle effects lets me avoid using mediums I'm not particularly skilled at. 
2. It lets me easily leverage programmatic methods of creating visuals--particle systems in particular can be manipulated into an endless variety of interesting effects with some programming.
3. A simple visual style tends to be highly legible, easy to convey information quickly, which complements the action gameplay nicely.

## Sea Salt City

[Link (itch.io)](https://ericzdw.itch.io/sea-salt-city)

Sea Salt City is a first-person shooter created in 10 days for Bigmode Game Jam 2026.

As the solo developer of the game, I created the concept, game design, programming, visuals, sound effects and music.

### Concept

One issue with game jams is that, once the jam is over, nearly all games in one are immediately lost media that no one will play. Considering this, some questions going into the game jam included, "How can I create something that I can look back on positively? "Can I create something that I can come back to again?"

The theme of the game jam was "Slick", which immediately brings to mind two aspects: things that are "cool", such as movements in action games, expressions of skill; another is the slipperiness of a "slick" material. Combined with the questions posed above, and that a player likely only has ten minutes to play the game, my concept going into the beginning of the jam revolved around an action game condensed in a "song" format. Like a song, the game would be short, but you can come back to it again anytime you want to experience it again. The best songs are ones you can listen to and be different after you listen to it for the rest of the day--this is one aspect I really aspired to capture.
